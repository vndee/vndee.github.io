Who am I?

I'm Duy Huynh, an AI/ML engineer & open-source builder

I'm a software engineer working across AI/ML and data, and an open-source builder at heart. My work lives at the intersection of AI and developer tooling — most of it local-first and built to run on your own machine. I created llm-sandbox, a lightweight runtime for safely running AI-generated code, now downloaded over 3.5 million times. I also write at blog.duy.dev about the things I build — from writing your own Redis to designing agentic-native engineering workflows around the terminal.
